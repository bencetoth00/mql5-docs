# Codes of Errors and Warnings

Codes of Errors and Warnings

This section contains the following descriptions:

- [Return codes of the trade server](Trade-Server-Return-Codes/Trade-Server-Return-Codes.md) – analyzing results of the [trade request](../Data-Structures/Trade-Request-Structure/Trade-Request-Structure.md) sent by function [OrderSend()](../../Trade-Functions/OrderSend/OrderSend.md);
- [Compiler warnings](Compiler-Warnings/Compiler-Warnings.md) – codes of warning messages that appear at compilation (not errors);
- [Compilation errors](Compilation-Errors/Compilation-Errors.md) – codes of error messages at an unsuccessful attempt to compile;
- [Runtime errors](Runtime-Errors/Runtime-Errors.md) – error codes in the execution of mql5-programs, which can be obtained using the [GetLastError()](../../Checkup/GetLastError/GetLastError.md) function.
