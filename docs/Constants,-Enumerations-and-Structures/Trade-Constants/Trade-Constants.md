# Trade Constants

Trade Constants

Various constants used for programming trading strategies are divided into the following groups:

- [History Database Properties](History-Database-Properties/History-Database-Properties.md) – receiving general information on a symbol;

- [Order properties](Order-Properties/Order-Properties.md) – obtaining information about trade orders;
- [Position properties](Position-Properties/Position-Properties.md) – obtaining information about current positions;
- [Deal properties](Deal-Properties/Deal-Properties.md) – obtaining information about deals;
- [Trade operation types](Trade-Operation-Types/Trade-Operation-Types.md) – description of trade operations available;
- [Trade transaction types](Trade-Transaction-Types/Trade-Transaction-Types.md) - description of possible trade transactions types;
- [Trade orders in DOM](Trade-Orders-in-DOM/Trade-Orders-in-DOM.md) – separation of orders according to the direction of a requested operation.
