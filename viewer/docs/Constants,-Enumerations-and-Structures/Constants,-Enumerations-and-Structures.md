# Constants, Enumerations and Structures


To simplify the program writing and to make program texts more convenient for perception, the MQL5 language provides predefined standard constants and enumerations. Besides that, service [structures](../Language-Basics/Data-Types/Structures,-Classes-and-Interfaces/Structures,-Classes-and-Interfaces.md) are used for storing information.

Standard constants are similar to macros and are of [int](../Language-Basics/Data-Types/Integer-Types/Char,-Short,-Int-and-Long-Types/Char,-Short,-Int-and-Long-Types.md) type.

The constants are grouped by their purposes:

- [Chart constants](Chart-Constants/Chart-Constants.md) are used when working with price charts: opening, navigation, setting parameters;
- [Objects constants](Objects-Constants/Objects-Constants.md) are intended for processing graphical objects that can be created and displayed in charts;
- [Indicators constants](Indicator-Constants/Indicator-Constants.md) are used for working with standard and custom indicators;

- [Environment state](Environment-State/Environment-State.md) constants describe properties of a MQL5-program, show information about a client terminal, financial instrument and current account;

- [Trade constants](Trade-Constants/Trade-Constants.md) allow to specify a variety of information in the course of trading;
- [Named constants](Named-Constants/Named-Constants.md) are constants of the MQL5 language;
- [Data structures](Data-Structures/Data-Structures.md) describe data storage formats used;
- [Codes of errors and warnings](Codes-of-Errors-and-Warnings/Codes-of-Errors-and-Warnings.md) describe compiler messages and trading server answers to trade requests;
- [In/out constants](Input-Output-Constants/Input-Output-Constants.md) are designed for working with [file functions](../File-Functions/File-Functions.md) and displaying messages on the screen by the [MessageBox()](../Common-Functions/MessageBox/MessageBox.md) function.
